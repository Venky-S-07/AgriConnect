## 1. Milestone Overview

Milestone 2 delivered the core **Farmer Context Store** for AgriConnect, establishing the foundational data layer required for all future agronomic intelligence, irrigation calculations, and weather risk matching.

### Delivered Capabilities:
- **Development Authentication & Session Handling:** Fast, secure mock OTP (`123456`) login with 10-digit Indian phone validation, returning a minimal JWT access token (24-hour expiration) containing strictly `sub` and `exp`.
- **Consent Governance (DPDP Scaffolding):**
  - Mandatory developmental data-processing consent for new farmer registration with timestamp and policy version (`v1.0-dev`).
  - Independent optional crop image training consent (unchecked by default).
  - Authenticated consent withdrawal and re-enablement via `PATCH /api/v1/auth/consent`.
  - Consent guard (`require_active_consent`) enforcing HTTP 403 on operational modifications while preserving HTTP 200 on read-only endpoints.
- **Farm Parcel Management (CRUD):**
  - Schema with parcel name, coordinates (latitude/longitude), area (acres), soil type, water source, water availability, and irrigation method.
  - Rigorous cross-field consistency validation (rainfed consistency, pump rate provenance enforcement).
  - Support for coordinate updates and rainfed farms (`None (Rainfed)`).
  - Full CRUD operations with tenant-isolated queries.
- **Regional Crop Catalog & Cultivation Lifecycle:**
  - Curated regional crops catalog (`crops_catalog.json`) covering 10 Indian crops with localized English and Kannada labels, typical durations, and growth stages.
  - Sowing date validation (past horizon <= 730 days, no future dates).
  - Growth stage validation against catalog stages.
  - Single active crop per farm policy (HTTP 409 conflict).
  - Crop stage update and soft deactivation endpoints with `crop_record_id` path identifier.
- **Privacy & Self-Service Data Governance:**
  - Self-service data export (`GET /api/v1/privacy/export`) returning structured JSON of farmer profile, consent audit history, farms, and crops — with strict exclusion of security secrets or OTPs.
  - Permanent account deletion (`DELETE /api/v1/privacy/delete-account`) triggering complete cascade deletion.
- **SQLite Dialect-Gated Foreign Key Enforcement:**
  - Connection listener attached directly to the SQLite engine instance:
    ```python
    if engine.dialect.name == "sqlite":
        @event.listens_for(engine, "connect")
        def set_sqlite_pragma(dbapi_connection, connection_record):
            cursor = dbapi_connection.cursor()
            cursor.execute("PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON;")
            cursor.close()
    ```
  - Verifiably cascades deletion from Farmer $\rightarrow$ Farms $\rightarrow$ Crops.
- **Bilingual Mobile-First Frontend:**
  - Authentication modal with mock OTP and consent toggles.
  - Multi-step Farm Setup Wizard (parcel infrastructure, optional GPS auto-detection with manual coordinate entry, crop selection and growth stage picker).
  - Farmer Dashboard displaying registered parcels, active crop badges, and growth stage management.
  - Full English and Kannada localization.
